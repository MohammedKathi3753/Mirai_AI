# 🤖 Mirai AI

## Adaptive AI Interview Coaching Platform

Mirai AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help candidates prepare for technical, HR, and behavioral interviews through personalized practice, intelligent feedback, and continuous performance tracking.

Unlike traditional interview-preparation platforms that primarily provide questions and scores, Mirai AI is being developed around an **adaptive coaching approach**:

> **Identify weaknesses → provide targeted practice → evaluate improvement → adapt future interviews.**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Interview preparation is often repetitive and generic.

Candidates commonly face problems such as:

- Practicing questions that do not match their target role
- Receiving generic feedback instead of actionable guidance
- Difficulty identifying their actual interview weaknesses
- Lack of personalized practice
- No structured way to measure improvement
- Difficulty preparing according to their resume and target job

Mirai AI aims to address these problems through personalized, AI-assisted interview coaching.

---

## 💡 Solution

Mirai AI is being developed as an **adaptive interview coach** rather than simply an AI question generator.

The planned system will:

1. Understand the candidate's profile and target role
2. Analyze the candidate's resume and job requirements
3. Generate relevant interview questions
4. Evaluate candidate responses
5. Identify specific weaknesses
6. Generate targeted practice activities
7. Adapt follow-up questions based on previous responses
8. Track improvement across multiple sessions
9. Provide an evolving interview-readiness profile
